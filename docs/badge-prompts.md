# Prompt gambar badge agen

Satu prompt dasar, tinggal ganti bagian subjek. Hasil dipakai di halaman Pencapaian; badge yang belum diraih dibuat abu-abu lewat CSS, jadi cukup satu gambar per badge.

## Satu prompt untuk semua badge (sprite sheet 5x5)

```
A sprite sheet of 25 separate badge emblems arranged in a perfect 5 by 5 grid, equal spacing, identical size, each fully separated with generous empty margin around it. Every badge is the same design family: a thick rounded frosted translucent glass medallion, soft 3D, subtle glossy rim light, with a raised icon at its center, one saturated red (#C4141B) accent part on each icon, dark warm-black (#1A1512) details, gentle iridescent reflection in red and cream tones only. Soft studio lighting from top-left, soft contact shadow below each badge. Seamless warm cream (#F4EFE6) background, front-facing and symmetrical, simple bold silhouettes that still read at 64px, square 1:1. Rounded friendly forms, no text, no letters, no numbers, no people, no logos. Premium, minimal, calm. Icons, left to right, top to bottom:
Row 1: (1) a small pointed wizard hat resting on a tidy stack of documents with a red ribbon seal; (2) a magnifying glass over an open book; (3) a rounded ticket with a star-shaped cutout; (4) a rounded wallet with a card peeking out; (5) a calendar page with one day circled and a checkmark seal.
Row 2: (1) five small round suitcases in a tidy cluster; (2) a small passenger van seen from the front with softly glowing windows; (3) a chunky rounded tour bus with a tiny crown on its roof; (4) a stack of three rounded ingot bars with a bright cream shine; (5) a rounded airplane climbing above soft clouds.
Row 3: (1) a bullseye target with a single arrow in the center; (2) a rounded broom with a trail of sparkles; (3) a crown floating above a short line of three tiny buses; (4) a friendly round speech bubble with a small sparkle; (5) a rounded butterfly with glass wings.
Row 4: (1) a small campfire flame; (2) a larger bright flame with a glowing core; (3) a rounded calendar wrapped by a continuous looping ribbon; (4) two interlinked rings; (5) three interlinked rings.
Row 5: (1) a cluster of five tiny rounded houses; (2) a small seedling growing from a glass cup, with one red leaf; (3) a graduation cap resting on an open book; (4) a trophy cup on a three-step podium; (5) a rounded star medal with a short ribbon and a small lightning bolt.
```

Urutan: kiri ke kanan, atas ke bawah, sama dengan urutan di bawah ini. Potong per kotak setelah jadi.
